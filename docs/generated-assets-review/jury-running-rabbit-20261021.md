# Jury and running Rabbit checkpoints

These two scoped adapters retain their complete original drawings until every
required image decodes. They are not global registry replacements. Source
provenance is recorded in `jury-provenance.json` and
`running-rabbit-provenance.json`; raw masters remain outside the repository.

## Jury

The generated decoration contains exactly twelve distinct jurors and their bench,
without painted slates. The twelve original `tr__slate` groups remain verbatim,
including their data coordinates, rectangle geometry, scribble, check/cross paths
and runtime-appended Witnesses pennies. No duplicated interactive groups exist.
Only their parent groups receive a ready-only y translation: 24 logical pixels
for even indices and 10 for odd indices. Before loading or after failure, those
parents have no transform, so the original composition stays unchanged.

The 1852×849 source is fitted to width480 and its visible ground at source y780
is aligned with logical y220. Offline SVG composition and independent review
confirmed twelve slates below the eyes and noses. Mouse slates touch the lower
chin without hiding the mouth. All remain within the existing jury button band;
the buttons, verdict handlers, slate data and penny construction were not edited.
The offline proof intentionally shows both mark paths at once because it omits
scene CSS. It is not evidence of the runtime initial state.

Enabled consumers: engraved Trial and Witnesses only. Other scenes and flat/paper
styles retain the original drawing. Three focused unit contracts pass.

## Running Rabbit

The white Rabbit remains inside the existing outer SVG, allowing Mouse's Tale
running animation and the other scenes' whole-figure bounce/mirror to continue.
A fixed120×100 paint rectangle preserves the animation box; the unchanged
1402×1122 source is width-fitted and source sole y1067 maps to logical y98.
A source inspection found four limbs, two ears and one tail, without an extra
watch. The existing Riverbank watch remains a separate interactive button.

Enabled consumers: engraved Riverbank, Rabbit Hole, Pool of Tears and Mouse's
Tale. The Trial evidence illustration and flat/paper styles remain original.
Five focused contracts pass. Ear/whisker margins, real watch contact and movement
at phone/desktop sizes remain visual checkpoints, not browser-tested claims.

## Limits

Encoding changed format only: WebP quality92, original dimensions, exact alpha.
No delivery-size optimization or performance-budget pass is claimed. All
browser playback, reduced motion, real button/penny rendering, phone/desktop and
both protagonist variants remain unverified for the new tree.
