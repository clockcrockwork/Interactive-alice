# Independent whole-pose consumers

These adapters are intentionally narrower than their registry IDs. They do not
flatten an animated rig or work around a refused separated-part generation.
Every required image must decode before the complete original drawing is hidden.
Both alternate art treatments and unrelated scenes keep their original paths.

## Falling Alice cards

The coherent yellow and blue whole masters serve only Drink Me's floating card
and Trial's exiting figure. Those consumers animate the outer figure, not its
independent limbs. The 971×1619 sources retain the fixed120×200 paint box, align
visible soles to y200 and preserve alpha exactly during WebP encoding.

Rabbit Hole continues its original head/hair/skirt/arm/leg rig. No refused leg
cutout is recreated. Duchess's separate shoulder/Flamingo contacts also remain
original rather than being presumed valid with the new body proportions. Five
focused tests cover both colours, both permitted scenes and excluded consumers.
Provenance: `src/assets/images/falling-card-provenance.json`.

## Duchess flying piglet

The Duchess cutaway moves the whole piglet through the sky on separately animated
paper wings. It has no Kitchen stage0–3 morph controls. The non-refused coherent
piglet master is therefore used only in that cutaway, inside its original200×200
box. The original external paper wings, flight path, rotation, pause and cut-off
remain unchanged. Two focused contracts protect this scope and load fallback.

An offline three-state SVG composition used the unchanged source PNG and original
wing artwork/coordinates. The roots remain behind the body; the wings are
intentionally flat paper, as the original scene describes. The proof export's
upper edge cuts the most raised wings; this is not runtime clipping evidence.
The Kitchen transformation remains the original multi-part vector, not a two-frame
whole-image replacement. Provenance: `src/assets/images/flying-pig-provenance.json`.

## Unaccepted running-away hybrid

The available generated body/arms were trial-composed with the original unedited
leg and sole SVGs at rest and both stride extremes. The flat thin stockings and
elliptical shoes read as separated from the engraved dress; raised-leg states
leave excessive blank space below the hem. No runtime switch is enabled for this
candidate, no new leg image is made, and the original running-away rig remains.
This specific failed hybrid does not block the independent whole-pose cards.

## Verification boundary

Source and offline composition inspection are not browser execution. Phone and
desktop framing, real playback, colour preference and delivery-size performance
remain checkpoints. No art sign-off, E2E pass or production readiness is claimed.
